# "Should YOU Pick Individual Stocks?" — YouTube Video Script

**Target length:** ~8:00
**Format:** Fully animated explainer (motion graphics, flat/2.5D style)
**Rule:** Every frame ≤ 7 seconds. One narration line = one frame.
**Suggested visual style:** Dark navy background, single accent color (green for "for", red for "against", amber for neutral facts). Consistent character: a simple stick-figure investor named "Alex". Numbers always animate by counting up/down, never static.

## DIRECTOR'S PRODUCTION NOTES

**Pacing & rhythm**
- 75 frames / ~480 seconds ≈ 6.4s average per frame — safely under the 7s cap.
- Scenes 4 and 5 (the "against" case) carry the most information; keep animations there minimal and let the numbers be the star. Scenes 3 and 6 can be more playful.
- Hard cuts at frames 18 → 19 and 43 → 44 are intentional tonal pivots — punch them with sound design (bass drop / record scratch).

**Visual system**
- Color grammar: GREEN = pro-picking, RED = anti-picking, AMBER = data/neutral, GOLD = the rare winners (frames 25–30, reuse the gold dots as a recurring motif).
- Recurring motifs to build payoff: the scales (fr. 12, 18, 69–71), the fork in the road (fr. 6, 62), the INDEX cube (fr. 20, 30), the gold dots (fr. 25, 27, 30, 66).
- Alex never speaks; all emotion via body language. Keep Alex identical in every frame for continuity.

**Data & sources shown on screen (small corner tags)**
- Frame 21–22: SPIVA U.S. Scorecard (S&P Dow Jones Indices) — ~90% of large-cap active funds underperform over 15 years.
- Frame 24–26: Bessembinder, "Do Stocks Outperform Treasury Bills?" (2018) — ~4% of stocks account for all net wealth creation; the median stock underperformed T-bills.
- Frame 40: "behavior gap" — investor vs. investment returns (e.g., Morningstar "Mind the Gap").
- Frame 42: Barber & Odean, "Trading Is Hazardous to Your Wealth" (2000).
- Add an on-screen disclaimer in the outro: "Not financial advice. For education only."

**Voice-over direction**
- Conversational, confident, slightly wry. Slow down 10% on frames 21, 25, 54, 73 — those are the quotable lines.
- Target ~140 words per minute; total VO is ~1,100 words ≈ 7:50 with breathing room for stings.

**Thumbnail ideas**
1. Split face: half genius (green chart) / half gambler (red slot machine), text "90% LOSE".
2. One gold dot among 96 gray dots, text "Only 4% of stocks matter".
